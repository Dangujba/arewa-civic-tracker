# Arewa Civic Tracker — Brand Guidelines

Approved visual identity update: 3 November 2026. Based on the exact logo supplied by the project owner (the black/copper Arewa Civic Tracker artwork with a phone, map pin, green checklist ticks and tagline).

## Logo applications

- **Official full logo**: use on public landing page, login, presentation materials, stakeholder PDFs, email header, and Android launch/sign-in.
- **Compact wordmark**: cropped from the original approved image; use in the staff sidebar, small web headers and public transparency header. Never redraw the wordmark in a mismatched font.
- **Brand mark**: crop the phone/checklist mark from the same approved image; use as favicon, Android adaptive icon and other very small placements. Do not scale the full small print into an app icon.
- Maintain natural aspect ratio, avoid adding drop shadows, gradients, round containers or stretching.
- Keep white or warm ivory clear space around the art. Preserve the original logo's green checks and red map pin; these are not general interface primary colors.
- If an asset is replaced or regenerated, maintain its origin as the user-approved uploaded artwork. Do not use unrelated stock graphics or re-create a near-copy.

## Semantic palette

| Token | Hex | Intended use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Primary copper | #9E5D37 | Main actions, navigation, selected controls, links |
| Dark copper | #773F24 | Hover and strong accent backgrounds |
| Deep copper | #62331D | Pressed states and strong contrast |
| Charcoal | #171717 | Titles and body text |
| Warm white | #FAF8F5 | Application background |
| White | #FFFFFF | Cards and forms |
| Soft copper | #F6EBE3 | Selected navigation backgrounds and highlights |
| Copper border | #E0C8B7 | Focus and component borders |
| Success green | #087640 | Confirmed successful actions only |
| Error red | #DC2626 | Errors, destructive actions and alerts |
| Warning brown | #805021 | Pending/needs review |

Primary copper #9E5D37 has approximately 5.1:1 white contrast; use white text on copper primary buttons. Do not use white on pale copper backgrounds. Red and green must not be the sole signals for state; include text/icons.

## UI application

- Flat, angular cards and buttons. Border radius 0 for business controls. No ornamental shadows or excessive padding.
- Navigation selected state is soft copper with dark copper text and a copper left border.
- Standard buttons have white text on solid copper; hover dark copper. Disabled state must be visibly different.
- Form focus rings and searchable dropdown selection are copper. Input surfaces remain white.
- Badges represent semantics: approved/success stays green, rejected/error stays red, pending remains warning brown. Brand color is not a substitute for status.
- Reports, exports and email keep a consistent header logo, copper rule, charcoal typography, transparent paper backgrounds, and readable evidence.
- Android native uses copper for app bar, primary CTA and active navigation, with flat square shapes. Adaptive launcher artwork must originate from the approved logo.
- Accessibility: check contrast, text scaling, touch targets, and focus outlines after style changes.

## Release verification

Check homepage, portal header/sidebar, login, reporting forms and Ward selection, searchable tables, report show, PDF/print, public transparency/map, email notifications and Android. Do not declare APK deployed before build/signature validation and website download checks.
